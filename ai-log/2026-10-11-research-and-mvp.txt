    with sources, why SPY and VOO are usually unavailable to EU retail investors
    under the PRIIPs rules. It then compares European (UCITS) funds that track
    the same indexes. It makes no profit promises and says clearly that it isn't
    investment advice.
  - Lead magnet: a PDF cheat sheet with the comparison table and a 7-point
    checklist for choosing an ETF in a broker app. The download appears right
    after the form is submitted.
  - Ads: Variant A leads with the problem ("your broker won't let you buy
    SPY?"). Variant B leads with the comparison ("4 ETFs available to Czechs in
    one overview").
  - Funnel tracking: page view → comparison seen → button click → form started →
    submitted → lead saved → PDF downloaded. UTM tags from the ad are saved in
    the browser session and sent as hidden form fields, so each lead shows which
    ad it came from.
  - Tech: plain HTML, CSS and a little JavaScript, with no build step. Hosting
    on Netlify's free tier, using its built-in form handling for storage and
    spam protection, plus a cookie-free analytics tool.

    according to an ICE press release).
  - The KID rule: the EU regulation (PRIIPs, Articles 5 and 13) requires a KID
    before a retail purchase. That US ETF issuers generally don't provide one
